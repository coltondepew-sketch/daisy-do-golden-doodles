A little about us

Raised in our home. Chosen for yours.

We’re a family in Southern Alberta raising Daisy’s Goldendoodle puppies with hands-on care and daily family interaction. Our goal isn’t simply to find homes — it’s to match each puppy with a family where they can thrive. We work with families throughout Alberta, including Calgary and Edmonton, and into British Columbia and Western Canada. For the right home, we’re happy to discuss meeting or delivery arrangements.

Questions

Frequently asked

When can the puppies go home?

The litter was born July 31, 2026 and will be ready to leave at about 8 weeks old, around September 25, 2026.

How big will they be?

We expect adult sizes to generally fall around 30–55 lb, although every puppy is different.

What will their coats be like?

This litter may include straight, wavy and curly coats in colours ranging from light golden to deeper red tones.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Start with our puppy application. We review it, connect with you, confirm availability and help you choose a puppy that fits your family. Reservation details are then confirmed directly with you.

How we raise our puppies

Raised with our family from the very beginning.

Our puppies grow up with everyday family interaction, handling, play and affection. We also begin introducing them to a designated potty area to give their new families a head start when continuing potty training at home.

Early potty training

We introduce the puppies to a designated potty area while they are still with us, helping them begin learning that there is a specific place to go.
