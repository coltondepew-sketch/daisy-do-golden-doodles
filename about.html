Our family, our dogs

Daisy was our family dog first.

Daisy Do started with one very special dog — Daisy — and a love for the kind of companionship a great family dog brings into everyday life.

Everything grew from Daisy.

Daisy came into our lives as a puppy and quickly became part of everything we did. With five kids in the house, she grew up surrounded by activity, affection and plenty of adventure.

She came camping with us, explored the mountains, spent summer days at the water and, whenever she had the chance, went swimming. We fell in love with her gentle personality, intelligence and the way she fit so naturally into family life.

“Daisy was our family dog first. Everything else grew from there.”
Why Daisy Do?

We wanted other families to experience what Daisy gave ours.

Watching Daisy grow into such a loving, smart and dependable family dog is what planted the idea of raising puppies. We chose Rigby with health, temperament and compatibility in mind, and both Daisy and Rigby have completed genetic health testing through Embark.

Our puppies are raised as part of our family here in Southern Alberta. From the beginning they are handled, loved, played with and introduced to the everyday sights and sounds of a busy family home. Our goal is not simply to place puppies — it is to give them a great beginning and find families who will make them part of their lives the way Daisy became part of ours.

Our puppies are raised the same way Daisy was — as part of the family.
